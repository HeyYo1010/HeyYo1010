I build AI systems that make it into production. Most of my work sits between the model and
the user: retrieval pipelines, multi-agent orchestration, evaluation, and the interfaces that
make any of it usable.

Coming from full stack means I can take something from prototype to shipped without waiting
on a handoff. Useful, in a field where the hard part is rarely the model.
